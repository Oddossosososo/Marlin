HOW TO READ THIS

Time is the main speed comparison: lower is faster. NPS means searched nodes per second, but node counts are not directly equivalent between different search algorithms. Both engines are asked to complete the same depth; their evaluation and pruning methods differ. This is a browser/device benchmark, not an Elo rating or a playing-strength test. Results can vary with device load, browser, and thermal throttling.
